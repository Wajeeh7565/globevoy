How Globevoy calculates this
FlightEstimated from partner flight data (Aviasales / Travelpayouts). Falls back to a route model when live data isn't available.
StayEstimated from partner stay data (Agoda / Travelpayouts). Falls back to nightly averages for the city.
Daily spendFood, transit, and small extras from Globevoy's travel cost estimates for this city.
Local funAllowance for tours and entry tickets, sized to typical local prices.
Budget FitCompares the total estimate with your budget so you see real headroom, not just airfare.
