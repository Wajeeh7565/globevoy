HOW IT WORKS

One trip story, built around your real budget.

Globevoy turns an origin, a total group budget and your timing into connected trip options. You can compare the plan here, then complete each booking with the relevant partner.

1

Tell us the trip shape

Choose where you are leaving from, your total budget for the travelling group and when you would like to go.

2

Compare connected options

Review route order, nights, transport, stays and the evidence behind each price without losing the complete-trip context.

3

Confirm with partners

Globevoy hands you to external flight and hotel partners. Final prices, availability and booking conditions are confirmed there.

Why estimates can change

Travel inventory moves quickly. Globevoy labels live, cached and estimated evidence separately and does not claim a planning estimate is a bookable fare.
