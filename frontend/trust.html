TRUST AND PRICING

Know what is observed, cached and estimated.

A useful travel plan must be honest about its evidence. Globevoy keeps planning estimates separate from provider-backed observations and sends final confirmation to the booking partner.

L

Live provider evidence

A provider observation returned for the selected route and dates. It is still subject to availability and final confirmation on the partner website.

C

Cached observation

A previously observed provider amount. It helps with planning but is not described as live or current.

E

Planning estimate

A directional amount used to shape the trip. It is not a quoted fare and should not be treated as bookable evidence.

Partner checkout remains separate

Globevoy does not issue tickets, reserve rooms, collect payment or promise a one-click complete-trip purchase. Partner websites control their own inventory, fees, conditions and checkout.
