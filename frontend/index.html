How Globevoy calculates this

Every estimate, sourced and labelled.

Each part of the trip total is labelled so you can see what comes from partner data and what uses a fallback estimate.

Partner data

Flight estimate

Pulled from partner flight data (Aviasales / Travelpayouts). Falls back to a route model when live data isn't available.

Partner data

Hotel / stay estimate

Pulled from partner stay data (Agoda / Travelpayouts). Falls back to nightly averages by city when needed.

Globevoy's travel cost estimates

Daily spend

Food, transit and small extras for the city you're heading to, kept up to date in Globevoy's travel cost estimates.

Globevoy's travel cost estimates

Local activities

A small allowance for tours and entry tickets, sized to the city's typical local cost.

Budget Fit

Budget Fit score

Compares the full estimate with your real budget so you see how much room you have, not just the cheapest flight.
