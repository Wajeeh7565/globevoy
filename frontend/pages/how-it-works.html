Three useful ways to compare trips

LOWEST COST

Prioritizes the lowest realistic trip estimate while still meeting the selected trip requirements.

BEST BALANCE

Balances estimated cost, convenience, travel time, stay value and overall destination fit.

MOST MEMORABLE

Prioritizes a more distinctive destination or route experience while remaining within the planning limit.

A higher estimated cost should provide visible user value. It is not automatically a better recommendation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I book directly on Globevoy? expand_more
No. Globevoy helps users discover, compare and plan. Final booking and payment take place on partner websites.
Why do you reserve 10% of my budget? expand_more
We believe in realistic travel planning. The 10% reserve acts as a buffer for personal spending, meals, local transit, or unexpected minor fees, ensuring you don't overspend your total intended budget just on flights and hotels.
Are the prices shown guaranteed? expand_more
No. Some values may come from provider results, while others may be cached or planning estimates. Travel prices and availability can change. Final values are confirmed on the partner website.
How does Multi-city planning work? expand_more
Globevoy compares eligible city combinations and builds a connected route that includes outbound travel, every stay, inter-city transport and the return journey.
How do you select stay recommendations? expand_more
We evaluate accommodations based on a combination of factors: cost relative to your remaining budget, location centrality, and aggregate guest ratings. We present options categorized by 'Best balance', 'Best value', etc.
Is Globevoy free to use? expand_more
Globevoy currently presents travel discovery and planning tools without direct checkout. Any future fees, account benefits or referral relationships should be clearly disclosed.
