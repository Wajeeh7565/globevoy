Design reference: Trust & Evidence · MVP now · not connected to production data.

Travel planning should show its evidence.

Globevoy separates provider results, cached data and planning estimates so travellers can understand what is known, what may change and what must still be confirmed.

Final availability and prices are confirmed on partner websites.

Trust Principles

search

Clear Evidence

We identify whether a value comes from a provider result, cached provider data or a planning estimate.

info

Complete Context

We show the major cost components used to evaluate a trip, including flights, stays and inter-city transport when applicable.

gpp_maybe

Honest Limits

We explain when information is incomplete, unavailable or awaiting partner confirmation.

account_balance_wallet

User-Controlled Budget

We keep the protected reserve visible and separate from the estimated trip cost.

verified
