Information users provide

When you use Globevoy to plan your budget-first travels, we may process information that you explicitly provide to us. This is necessary to curate and deliver personalized travel discovery experiences.

  • Search Parameters: Origin locations, destination preferences, travel dates, and budget constraints.
  • Account Information: When account features are enabled, and if you choose to create an account, we may collect your email address, name, and saved preferences.
  • Communications: Any information you provide when contacting our support team or providing feedback.

Information generated through use

As you interact with our platform, we automatically generate and collect data regarding your usage patterns to improve our curation algorithms and ensure evidence-led recommendations.

  • Interaction Data: The specific travel recommendations you view, save, or dismiss.
  • Curation Preferences: Implicit preferences derived from the types of role-based travel options (e.g., 'Best Balance') you frequently engage with.

Search and recommendation records. For each search we keep a record of the trips we recommended and what you did with them (viewed, opened, shown other trips, followed a partner booking link). We use these records to improve and test our recommendations. The record holds your departure airport, a budget range rather than the exact amount, the number of travellers and a random ID. If you are signed in, the ID is a one-way code made from your account, never your name or email. These records are deleted automatically after 13 months, and when you delete your account.

Technical information

We automatically collect technical data from your device to ensure the platform functions correctly and securely.

This includes your IP address, browser type and version, time zone setting, browser plug-in types and versions, operating system and platform, and other technology on the devices you use to access this website.

How information is used

Globevoy uses the collected information strictly to provide and improve our services, maintaining our commitment to a calm and credible user experience.

  • To generate data-driven, budget-conscious travel recommendations.
  • To distinguish between live, cached, and estimated data for accurate presentation.
  • To maintain and improve the security of our platform.
  • To analyze aggregated usage trends without identifying individual users.

Cookies and Analytics

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to track activity on our platform and hold certain information. Cookies are files with a small amount of data which may include an anonymous unique identifier.

We prioritize essential cookies required for core functionality. Analytics cookies are used sparingly to understand general usage trends, not to build extensive personal profiles.

Partner Links

Our platform may contain links to third-party travel providers. If you interact with these links, information may be processed by those third parties according to their own privacy policies. Globevoy is not responsible for the privacy practices of these external sites.
