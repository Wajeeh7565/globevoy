Travel discovery should begin with what you can spend.

Globevoy is a budget-first travel discovery product that helps travellers compare realistic Single-destination and Multi-city possibilities before committing to a destination.

Our Mission

To make travel discovery clearer, more realistic and more useful by starting with the traveller’s complete budget.

Traditional travel planning often starts in the wrong place

Most tools ask where you want to go before knowing what you can afford. This leads to frustrating discoveries deep into the planning process.

location_off

Destination first

Picking a destination without knowing flight and hotel costs often leads to abandoning the idea later when reality sets in.

account_balance_wallet

Fragmented costs

Looking at flights on one site and hotels on another makes it hard to see the total budget impact of a decision.

filter_list_off

Too much choice

Endless lists of slightly different options create decision fatigue instead of clarity on what matters most.

A clearer way to discover trips

We reversed the process. Tell us your budget, dates, and origin, and we show you where you can go and stay within that number.

Stays

Curated hotel options that fit the remaining budget after flights.

What guides the product

Budget first

Every suggestion must fit within the user's stated budget before it is shown.

Protected reserve

We always leave a portion of the budget unallocated for daily expenses and surprises.

Curated choice

We show a limited number of high-quality options rather than an overwhelming list.

Visible trade-offs

We make it clear when spending more on a flight means spending less on a hotel.

Evidence labels

We explain why an option was selected (e.g., "Best flight value", "Central location").

Partner confirmation

We hand off to trusted partners for final booking to ensure reliable execution.

Familiar anchors and worthwhile discoveries

We believe the best trips combine places you know you want to see with places that offer surprising value along the way.

By suggesting hidden-value cities near familiar anchors, we help stretch your budget further while expanding your travel horizons.

Anchor Cities

Well-known destinations that often serve as primary hubs but may have higher costs.

Discovery Cities

Nearby locations that offer excellent cultural value and more affordable stays.

A hotel star category is not the whole experience

We look beyond basic star ratings to curate stays based on location quality, historical reliability, and how well they fit the specific needs of your itinerary.
