# Search activity disclosure - approval required

Applicable public notice: https://globevoy.com/privacy.html, source frontend/pages/privacy.html, linked by frontend/scripts/shell-670f.js. It is currently a June 2026 draft/design reference with broad device/IP/cookie and recommendation statements. This is not a finalized reviewed notice. Do not represent it as adequate legal approval or delete infrastructure/account disclosures merely because the new event dataset excludes identities.

Proposed text for the notice's generated-information, analytics and retention sections, requiring owner/legal review and approved publication before activation:

"When search analytics is enabled, GlobeVoy records coarse backend search actions to understand reliability and usage. These records contain a UTC date, request-specific random identifier, search category, outcome, result-count range, duration range and price-evidence category. They do not contain your name, email, account or session identifier, IP address, raw query, travel dates, chosen airports, itinerary or credentials. No analytics cookie or sign-in is required. These counts measure backend actions, not unique people, sessions, purchases or confirmed bookings. One visit may generate multiple initial searches, adjustments or calendar lookups, which we count separately.

We store these activity records in AWS in the United States (Ohio), with restricted operational access. Each UTC day's activity becomes eligible for automatic deletion 30 days after that day's midnight UTC. This means roughly 29 to 30 days before expiry, depending on the time of the action. The operational query tool excludes expired records immediately. Physical DynamoDB TTL deletion is asynchronous, typically occurring within a few days after expiry; we do not promise deletion at exactly 30 days. Account information, technical infrastructure logs and external referral partners have separate purposes and retention practices described elsewhere in this notice."
